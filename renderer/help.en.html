Kinoshift Help

Convert your audio and video files in a few clicks, right on your computer.

1. Add files

  • Drag and drop your files onto the dashed area.
  • Or click it, or use File > Add Files… (⌘O on Mac, Ctrl+O on Windows).

You can add several files: they are converted one after the other.

2. Choose the format

In “Convert to”, pick the result you want:

  • Audio: MP3, AAC (M4A), WAV, FLAC, OGG Vorbis. You can also choose a sample rate.
  • Video: MP4, WebM, MKV and animated GIF.
  • Devices: ready-made settings for iPhone, iPad, Apple TV, Android, Fire, Switch… (recent and older models).

3. Advanced options

Expand “Advanced options” to fine-tune:

  • Quality: economy, balanced or high.
  • Frame rate and resolution (video): the resolution is a maximum, video is never scaled up.
  • Audio track: pick the track or remove the sound.
  • Trim: enter a start and an end, for example 90, 1:30 or 1:02:03.

4. Output folder

By default, the converted file is saved next to the original. Click “Change…” to pick another folder, then “Reset” to go back to the default. An existing file is never overwritten: a number is added to the name, for example movie (1).mp4.

5. Convert

  • Click Convert (⌘↩ or Ctrl+Enter).
  • A progress bar is shown for each file.
  • Cancel stops a running conversion, Show reveals the result in Finder or Explorer, Remove removes the row from the list (not the file).
  • Clear finished tidies up the list.

FFmpeg

FFmpeg, the conversion engine, is included with the app: nothing to install. If the app still says “FFmpeg was not found”, use “Retry” or “Choose…” to point to it, or the Help > Install FFmpeg menu.

Appearance

The theme (system, light or dark) is set in the Kinoshift > Settings menu on Mac, or File > Settings on Windows.
